01 / About

A bit about me

I started out in finance, studying Accounting and Finance at IBA Karachi and then working in treasury operations at Meezan Bank, where getting a number wrong was never an option. From there I moved to Unilever, first running B2B operations and later working as a business data analyst for Unilever Food Solutions.

The project I'm proudest of from that time started with a frustrating daily routine: export files from SAP, rebuild the same Excel reports, send them around, repeat tomorrow. I replaced it with a Power BI dashboard that pulls the data in, calculates the KPIs for every sales manager and flags which products are running short in which distribution centers. It's now used across every category at Unilever.

At Purdue I've been pushing further into machine learning, simulation and AI systems, and I like work that sits right between the business question and the technical build. If a model doesn't change what someone does on Monday morning, I don't think it's finished yet.
